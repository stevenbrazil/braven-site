A look at the kind of tooling Braven Dynamics builds.

Below are some custom examples of tools I have created to help streamline the manufacturing process, reduce downtime, and increase productivity. Contact Braven Dynamics for any of your custom tooling needs.

OP2 Soft Jaws / Locating Fixture

3D-printed Nylon PA612-CF soft jaws designed to hold an odd-shaped part securely while keeping critical surfaces clear for machining. These can be created as a two-piece jaw or one-piece fixture. Possible hybrid tooling option to incorporate standard hardware.

Replacement outdated fence hinge

Water, UV, and chemical resistant ASA Hinge made for outdated, overpriced, or parts with long lead times. Also acts as a jig and fixture to temporarily hold fence together until new parts arrive. ASA is also impact resistant, making it tough in any weather.

Reverse Engineering

Getting correct parts can be a nightmare. Luckily with proper reverse engineering, getting parts made fast and affordable can sve a lot of time and trouble

Assembly support fixture

3D-printed support fixture that holds components in place during assembly, freeing up hands and improving consistency.

Inspection cradle

Cradle that positions parts in a repeatable orientation for inspection, making it easier to check key features.

Prototype tooling concept

Early-stage tooling used to test a new process before committing to more permanent fixtures or automation.
