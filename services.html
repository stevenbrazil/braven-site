Services

Tooling and design services built for real use.

From one-off fixtures to repeatable soft jaws and handling tools, Braven Dynamics focuses on practical, 3D-printed solutions that support your existing machines and processes.

Fixtures & jigs

Custom workholding

Fixtures and jigs designed around your parts, so operators can load, align, and run jobs with less trial-and-error and more consistency.

Soft jaws

3D-printed soft jaws

Soft jaws that protect delicate parts, improve access for tools, and make it easier to hold odd shapes without chewing them up.

EOAT & handling

End-of-arm tooling

Lightweight, custom end-of-arm tooling and handling fixtures for automation, pick-and-place, and repeatable part movement.

Prototype tooling

Test and refine

Early-stage tooling to prove out ideas, test setups, and refine how parts are held or moved before committing to more permanent solutions.

CAD support

Clean, usable models

Production-friendly CAD models for tooling and fixtures, built to be easy to understand, modify, and reuse later.

Custom requests

One-off problem solving

Have a weird part, awkward setup, or “we just need something to make this easier” situation? That’s exactly the kind of work Braven Dynamics is built for.

How projects typically work.

  • You share the part, problem, or process that needs support.
  • We discuss constraints: machines, space, access, and goals.
  • You receive a clear concept and timeline before anything is finalized.
  • Once approved, the tooling is built and prepared for use.
